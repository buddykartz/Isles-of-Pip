Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Isles of Pip does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address or location. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use a device advertising identifier, device and usage information and approximate location to show and measure ads, and to limit repeated ads. Where required, the game asks for your consent first, and on iOS it asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can decline, and you can change your choice in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. I do not receive your payment details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
