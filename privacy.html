Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Isles of Pip is a single-player puzzle game for a general audience. It has no accounts, no user-generated content and no server of its own. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The app does not ask for your name, email address or contact details.

Advertising and the data it uses

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (an optional rewarded ad for a hint, and short ads between some levels). To show and measure ads, limit repeats and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device and advertising identifiers (such as the iOS advertising identifier, if you allow tracking)
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from your network
  • ad interaction and product-usage information
  • crash, performance and other diagnostic data

This data is shared with Google for those purposes only. I do not receive it, I do not sell it, and I use no other analytics service. Where required, the game asks for your consent first (Google's consent form), and on iOS it asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can decline, and you can change your choice in the game's Settings (Privacy options) or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also limit ad personalisation in your device settings. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. I do not receive your payment details. Once you buy it, the game stops showing ads.

Children

Isles of Pip is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not in the Kids Category. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will help.

Your choices

You can decline tracking, change your consent choice, remove ads with the in-app purchase, or delete the game at any time, which removes everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
