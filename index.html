How do I remove ads?

Tap "No ads" on the main menu. It is a one-time purchase.

I bought it on another device.

Open Settings in the game and choose Restore Purchases.

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap the hint button in the level. Swipe sideways to turn the world: many routes only connect from the right angle.

Do I need to sign in to anything?

No. Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android are optional. They add a leaderboard for stars and achievements, and the game plays the same without them.
