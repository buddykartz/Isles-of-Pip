Isles of Pip

A calm isometric puzzle game: turn the world, bend the stairs and guide Pip home across 100 tiny isles.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email islesofpip@gmail.com and I'll reply as soon as I can. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I remove ads? Tap "No ads" on the main menu. It is a one-time purchase.

I bought it on another device. Open Settings in the game and choose Restore Purchases.

I'm stuck on a level. Tap the hint button in the level. Swipe sideways to turn the world: many routes only connect from the right angle.
